# Frequently Asked Questions
## Orders
### How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also check your order status by contacting customer support with your order ID.
### Can I modify my order after placing it?
Orders can be modified within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Contact support immediately if you need changes.
### Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After processing begins, cancellation is not guaranteed. Contact support as soon as possible.
## Payments
### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Aster & Row gift cards.
### Do you offer financing?
Yes, we offer Affirm financing on orders over $200. Choose Affirm at checkout.
## Account
### Do I need an account to order?
No, guest checkout is available. However, an account lets you track orders and save preferences.
