# Shipping Policy
## Domestic Shipping
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days, free on orders over $75
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days, $14.99
- Next-day shipping: 1 business day, $24.99 (order by 2pm EST)
## Furniture & Large Items
- White glove delivery: 10-14 business days, $49.99
- Curbside delivery: 7-10 business days, free on orders over $500
## International Shipping
- Canada: 7-14 business days, flat rate $19.99
- UK/EU: 10-21 business days, flat rate $29.99
- Australia: 14-28 business days, flat rate $39.99
- Duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient
## Tracking
All orders receive tracking numbers via email within 24 hours of shipment.
