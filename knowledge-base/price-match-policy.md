# Price Match Guarantee
## Policy
- We match prices from authorized retailers on identical items
- Price match requests must be made within 7 days of purchase
- The competing price must be currently available (not expired)
## Requirements
- Provide a link to the competing offer
- Item must be identical (same brand, model, color, size)
- Competitor must be an authorized retailer (no auction sites, marketplace sellers)
## Exclusions
- Clearance or closeout items
- Coupon or promo code prices
- Bundle deals
- Membership/loyalty pricing from competitors
## How to Request
Contact customer support with your order number and the competing offer link. We'll verify and apply the adjustment within 2-3 business days.
## Important
Price adjustments are issued as a credit to your original payment method. We do NOT offer additional percentage-off on top of price matching.
