# Holiday Extended Return Policy
## Extended Return Window
- All purchases made between November 1 and December 31 may be returned until January 31 of the following year
- This extends the standard 30-day return window
## Conditions
- All other return policy conditions still apply
- Furniture items: extended to 30 days instead of 14 days during holiday period
- Restocking fee for furniture is WAIVED during the holiday return period
## Important
This policy OVERRIDES the standard return policy for purchases made during the holiday period. The restocking fee waiver applies to ALL furniture purchased Nov 1 - Dec 31.
