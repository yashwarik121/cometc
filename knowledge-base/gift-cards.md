# Gift Card Policy
## Purchasing
- Available in $25, $50, $100, $200, and custom amounts
- Digital delivery via email
- No expiration date
## Redemption
- Redeemable on asterandrow.com
- Can be combined with other payment methods
- Cannot be used to purchase other gift cards
- Balance carries over if purchase is less than card value
## Returns
- Gift cards are non-refundable and non-returnable
- Items purchased with gift cards follow standard return policy
- Refunds for gift card purchases are issued as store credit
