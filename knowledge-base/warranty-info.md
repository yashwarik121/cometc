# Warranty Information
## Coverage
- Furniture: varies by product (see product listing)
- Decor items: 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects
- Textiles: 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects
## What's Covered
- Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- Structural failures under normal use
- Hardware malfunctions (drawer slides, hinges, etc.)
## What's Not Covered
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from misuse, accidents, or modifications
- Fading from sun exposure
- Damage from improper cleaning
## How to File a Claim
1. Contact customer support with your order number
2. Provide photos of the defect
3. Our team will review within 3-5 business days
4. If approved, we'll arrange repair or replacement
## Important Note
Warranty claims must be filed within the warranty period. Proof of purchase required.
