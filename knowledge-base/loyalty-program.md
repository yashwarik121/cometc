# Aster & Row Rewards Program
## How It Works
- Earn 1 point per $1 spent
- 500 points = $25 reward
- Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity
## Tiers
### Member (0-999 points)
- Earn 1 point per $1
- Free standard shipping on orders over $75
### Gold (1000-4999 points)
- Earn 1.5 points per $1
- Free standard shipping on all orders
- Early access to sales
### Platinum (5000+ points)
- Earn 2 points per $1
- Free express shipping on all orders
- Early access to new collections
- Birthday reward ($50 credit)
## Enrollment
Sign up for free at asterandrow.com/rewards or during checkout.
