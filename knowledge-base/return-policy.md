# Return & Refund Policy
## General Returns
- Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery
- Items must be unused, in original packaging
- Customer pays return shipping unless item is defective
## Refund Processing
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the return
- Refunds issued to original payment method
- Shipping charges are non-refundable
## Non-Returnable Items
- Custom/personalized items
- Final sale items (marked as such)
- Gift cards
- Items damaged by customer misuse
## Exchanges
- Exchanges available for size/color within 30 days
- Subject to availability
## Furniture Returns
- Furniture must be returned within 14 days
- A 15% restocking fee applies to furniture returns
- White glove return pickup can be arranged for $79.99
