# Shipping Policy
## Domestic Shipping
- Standard shipping: 7-10 business days, free on orders over $100
- Express shipping: 3-5 business days, $19.99
- No next-day shipping option available
## International Shipping
- Canada only: 14-21 business days, flat rate $29.99
- No other international destinations available
## Note
Free shipping threshold was $100. Express was 3-5 days at $19.99.
