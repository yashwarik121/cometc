# Size & Fit Guide
## Before You Buy
### Measuring Your Space
- Measure doorways, hallways, and stairways for delivery access
- Allow at least 3 inches of clearance on each side for moving furniture
- Consider ceiling height for tall pieces
### Room Planning Tips
- Living room: leave 18" between sofa and coffee table
- Bedroom: leave 24" on each side of the bed for walking
- Dining: allow 36" from table edge to wall for chair movement
## Delivery Access Requirements
- Minimum doorway width: 32" for most items
- Elevator minimum: items must fit in standard residential elevator
- If delivery access is restricted, contact us BEFORE ordering
